Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
Overview: ncertTotal does not require user accounts, emails, phone numbers, or passwords. We do not sell data or share individual information with third parties.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not collect personal identity information:

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or social media logins
  • No contacts, SMS, precise location data, or device identifiers
  • No financial, billing, or payment data

2. Feature Usage & Anonymous Analytics

We use lightweight, privacy-conscious telemetry (powered by PostHog) to understand how the app is used and maintain reliability:

  • Feature Usage: Aggregated actions such as which classes, subjects, or study tabs are opened, enabling us to know which sections need improvement.
  • Link & Resource Health: Anonymous signals when textbook links fail to load or timeout, so dead links can be repaired quickly.
  • Diagnostics: App version, operating system version, and anonymous crash stack traces to fix bugs.

All telemetry events are assigned a randomized anonymous identifier and cannot be linked back to individual identities.

3. Device Storage & Local Files

When you download a textbook chapter, exemplar, or sample paper, the PDF file is stored locally on your device storage for offline reading. ncertTotal does not read, access, or inspect any other documents or files on your device.

4. External Links to NCERT Portals

When you download or view a textbook, the app connects directly to official servers (including ncert.nic.in). Connections to these third-party servers are governed by their respective privacy terms and network security standards.

5. Policy Updates

Any adjustments to this policy will be reflected here with an updated date. Continued use of the application indicates acceptance of these practices.
