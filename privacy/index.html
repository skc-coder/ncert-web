Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026
Zero Account Requirement:
ncertTotal does not require user accounts, phone numbers, email addresses, or student logins. Your personal notes, bookmarks, and scores stay on your device.

1. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No student names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No private student notes or custom written answers.
  • No camera access, contacts, or location tracking.

2. Anonymous Product Analytics (PostHog)

To fix crashes and see which textbook chapters or papers have broken links, the app sends anonymous technical events (screen views, button taps, error logs) with a random anonymous device ID.

You can turn off all analytics at any time directly in the app under Settings > Privacy & Analytics.

3. Local Storage

Downloaded textbooks and chapter bookmarks are saved in private app storage on your device. You can clear downloads at any time from Settings > Storage & Cache.

4. Privacy vs. Terms

This document explains our data and privacy practices. For usage terms, licensing, and disclaimers, please see our separate Terms of Service.
