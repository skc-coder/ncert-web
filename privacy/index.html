Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
Zero Account Policy: ncertTotal does not require accounts, sign-ups, phone numbers, or passwords. We do not track individual personal identities, sell data, or monetize user profiles.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The app never accesses or collects:

  • Names, email addresses, contact lists, or social media accounts
  • Precise GPS location or personal messages
  • Financial, credit card, or payment details

2. Feature Usage & Anonymous Analytics

To keep the app fast and actively fix broken features, we gather privacy-respecting, anonymized telemetry (via PostHog):

  • Feature Usage: Tracking which features and study sections are actively used (e.g., textbook reader, exemplar solver, board papers, search filters) to understand user needs and guide development.
  • Link & Resource Health: Detecting failed chapter downloads or network connection timeouts so we can immediately update broken links.
  • Crash & Performance Diagnostics: Anonymous stack traces and device platform versions (Android version, tablet vs phone form factor) to resolve crashes.

All telemetry events are assigned a randomly generated UUID that is never linked to personal identities.

3. Device Storage & Local Files

When you download study materials for offline reading, files are saved locally on your device. The app only accesses its own dedicated storage sandbox and never reads personal photos, documents, or external folders.

4. Direct Connections to Official NCERT Servers

When loading official textbooks, requests stream directly from government portals (including ncert.nic.in). These network transfers are subject to official server security protocols.

5. Updates to this Policy

Any revisions to our privacy practices will be documented here with an updated timestamp.
